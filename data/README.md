# Data provenance

**All records in this folder are synthetic and illustrative.** They were written for this proof of concept and do not describe real projects, publications or research results.

- `projects.csv` - 14 fictional research projects (`SYN-P01` to `SYN-P14`) across two policy topics: *Urban climate adaptation* (`SYN-P01` to `SYN-P08`) and *Sustainable urban mobility* (`SYN-P09` to `SYN-P14`).
- `findings.csv` - 34 fictional findings (`SYN-F001` to `SYN-F034`), each linked to one project.

Conventions:

- Identifiers start with `SYN-` to mark them as synthetic. They are not European Commission, CORDIS or grant identifiers.
- Source documents (e.g. "Synthetic final report - SYN-P01") and source references (`SYN-SRC-...`) are placeholders. No URLs are included.
- Finding descriptions are worded as hypothetical results ("Hypothetical field measurements suggest...") and must not be cited as evidence.
- The `data_status` column on every record repeats the label "Synthetic - illustrative only".

The findings were designed to show different evidence types and some deliberate tensions between projects (for example, reflective surfaces lowering surface temperature while possibly increasing pedestrian heat exposure, or shared e-scooters replacing walking rather than car trips), which is where cross-project comparison is useful. The theme *Implementation and governance* appears in both topics to show that themes are filtered within the selected topic.

A production dataset would require real, verified sources with document-level references and links, documented selection criteria and curation records.
